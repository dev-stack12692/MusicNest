# Legal Disclaimer & Attribution

### Overview
**MusicNest** is a third-party client interface designed to interact with YouTube Music services. MusicNest operates strictly as a front-end streaming client and does not function as a content host, media repository, or digital distributor.

### Support Content Creators
MusicNest strongly advocates for supporting musicians, song producers, and official platforms:

> **We highly recommend subscribing to an official YouTube Music Premium membership.** Subscribing to YouTube Music ensures direct financial compensation to artists and content creators through official monetization programs, licensing deals, and playback royalties.

### Content Removal & Inquiries
Because MusicNest merely mirrors and streams media hosted externally by YouTube, any copyright infringement notices, DMCA requests, or takedown requests must be directed to the source content host (YouTube / Google LLC). Removal of source material from YouTube automatically reflects across MusicNest sessions.
