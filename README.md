## Overview

MusicNest is a free Android music app built for people who just want to press play and keep listening. No audio ads, no paywall. It comes with karaoke-style synced lyrics, smart recommendations and offline playback in a clean, modern interface.

It is made for Indian listeners first, so the home feed leans towards Hindi and regional music.

> [!NOTE]
> MusicNest is free to use, but it is **not open source**. This repository hosts the APK releases and the project docs. The source code is not published and all rights are reserved.

## Features

<details>
<summary><b>Streaming & Playback</b></summary>
<br>

- **No Audio Ads** - Press play and keep listening, uninterrupted.
- **Adaptive Streaming** - Audio quality adjusts to your connection.
- **Data Saver Mode** - Lower data use on mobile networks.
- **Background Playback** - Listen with the screen off or while using other apps.
- **Offline Playback** - Songs you play are cached on your device so they play again without internet.
- **Smart Audio Focus** - Calls and unplugged headphones pause and resume playback automatically.
- **Audio Output Picker** - Choose where your music plays, including Bluetooth devices.
- **Equalizer** - Sliders plus saved EQ profiles, right on the Now Playing screen.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Lyrics</b></summary>
<br>

- **Karaoke-Style Synced Lyrics** - Words fill in as the song plays.
- **Multiple Lyric Animations** - Choose your lyric style, glow and text size.
- **Multiple Lyric Providers** - Better matching and coverage across songs.
- **Sync Nudge** - Nudge the timing when a track is slightly off.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Discovery & Search</b></summary>
<br>

- **Made for India First** - Home feed leans towards Hindi and regional music.
- **Smart Home Feed** - Jump Back In, mood categories and daily mixes built from what you listen to.
- **Smart Recommendations** - Suggestions that adapt to your listening.
- **Fast Search** - Songs, artists and albums with suggestions and recent searches.
- **Voice Search** - Find songs hands-free with Nest Find.
- **Podcasts** - A dedicated tab next to music.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Accounts & Cloud Sync</b></summary>
<br>

- **Optional Sign-In** - Use Google or email. The player works without an account.
- **Cloud Sync** - Your library, playlists and preferences follow you across devices.
- **Backup & Restore** - Back up your local data and restore it anytime.
- **Privacy Controls** - Analytics opt-out and in-app data deletion in Settings.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Sharing & Listen Together</b></summary>
<br>

- **Share Songs & Playlists** - Send musicnest.me links that open right in the app.
- **Listen Together** - Queue songs with friends in real time using a room code.
- **Spotify Playlist Import** - Paste a playlist link and bring your tracks over.
- **Referral Rewards** - Invite friends and earn rewards.

</details>

## Installation & Setup

### Android Installation

Download the latest APK from the [Releases Page](https://github.com/dev-stack12692/MusicNest/releases/latest) or from **[musicnest.me](https://musicnest.me)**.

| Platform | Requirement | Get it |
| :--- | :--- | :---: |
| **Android** | Android 8.0 or newer | [**Latest APK**](https://github.com/dev-stack12692/MusicNest/releases/latest) |

When you install an APK directly, Android will ask you to allow installs from your browser or file manager (`Settings > Apps > Special app access > Install unknown apps`).

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Updating from a build older than v1.3.2:** v1.3.2 is signed with a new key. Uninstall the old MusicNest first, then install the latest APK. From v1.3.2 on, updates install in place and Cloud Sync keeps your library and preferences.
