# The Note From Me(Swapnendu)

**MusicNest — Ad-Free Music for Everyone 🎧**

I used to be a Spotify user. But over time, I started asking myself a simple question: *Why should I have to pay just to enjoy music without interruptions?*

Listening to music should be about enjoying your favorite songs, matching your mood, and escaping into the moment. But frequent ads can interrupt that experience, while subscriptions aren't affordable for everyone.

So I asked myself: **Why not build something of my own?**

I started building MusicNest — an app designed to let me enjoy music my way, without audio ads interrupting the experience.

But then I thought, why should this be just for me? Everyone deserves access to tools that make their everyday experiences better.

So I polished MusicNest and made it publicly available, allowing others to try it too.

MusicNest started as a personal solution to a problem I experienced. Now, it's a project I want to share with everyone who loves music.

No audio ads. No unnecessary complications. Just music, your way.

https://musicnest.me

*Built by a music lover, for music lovers.*
