# Privacy Policy for MusicNest

**Effective Date:** September 7, 2026

MusicNest operates on a strict **zero personal data collection** philosophy. We believe your listening history, playlists, and personal files belong entirely to you.

---

## 1. Information We Do Not Collect

- We do **not** create or require accounts to use core local player features.
- We do **not** collect, store, sell, or rent personally identifiable information (PII) such as your legal name, physical address, email address, or phone number.
- We do **not** harvest or transmit your local file lists, directory paths, or device audio library to any remote server.
- We do **not** employ telemetry trackers, cross-site trackers, or third-party behavioral advertising SDKs.

---

## 2. On-Device Storage & Local Processing

All data generated by your interaction with MusicNest stays locally on your device:
- **Playlists and History:** Custom playlists, recently played tracks, favorites, and queue states are stored exclusively inside your device’s local database (Room / SQLite).
- **Settings & Preferences:** Visual preferences, lyric styles, and queue configurations are kept in encrypted local app storage.
- **Local Audio Indexing:** When granted media access permissions, MusicNest scans device storage strictly to build an on-device index for playback. This data never leaves your device.

---

## 3. Third-Party Network Requests

When you stream content or use network-assisted features, MusicNest connects to external public services to retrieve necessary assets:
- **Lyrics Providers:** When a track begins playing, MusicNest queries configured lyric APIs (such as BetterLyrics) with the track title and artist name to retrieve timestamped text. No user IDs are sent.
- **AI Recommendation Engine:** If you choose to enable the AI Recommendation features, track titles and genre tags are sent to the designated AI inference endpoint to calculate suggestions.
- **Playlist Importers:** When importing public playlists via URL, the link is processed to read public track listings.

Please review the privacy policies of any third-party providers you configure within the app, as their handling of network IP addresses is governed by their respective terms.

---

## 4. Android System Permissions

MusicNest requests only the minimum permissions necessary to function:
- `READ_MEDIA_AUDIO` / `READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE`: To detect, scan, and play offline audio files on your device.
- `INTERNET`: To retrieve network audio streams, album art, and synchronized lyrics.
- `FOREGROUND_SERVICE` & `FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MEDIA_PLAYBACK`: To maintain uninterrupted background audio playback and system notification controls when the app is minimized or the screen is locked.
- `WAKE_LOCK`: To prevent audio stuttering when the CPU enters low-power sleep mode during playback.

---

## 5. Security

Because MusicNest does not maintain remote servers or databases containing your personal information, there is no centralized database vulnerable to external breaches. You retain full control over your local data and can wipe all cached data, history, and playlists at any time via Android Application Settings.

---

## 6. Updates to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in functionality or legal requirements. Updates will always be documented in this file with a revised "Effective Date."
