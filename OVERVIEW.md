# MusicNest 🎵

A lightweight, modern, ad-free music streaming and offline playback client built for Android. MusicNest combines smart AI-assisted music discovery, syllable-synchronized dynamic lyrics, and seamless queue management into a clean, mobile-first experience.

---

## ✨ Features

- **🧠 AI Recommendation System:** Context-aware suggestions that dynamically adapt to your listening habits and mood.
- **⚡ Smart Queue System:** Infinite auto-fill, drag-and-drop reordering, and duplicate-safe queue management.
- **🎤 Dynamic Synced Lyrics:** Real-time, word-by-word and syllable-by-syllable synchronized lyrics animations with customizable visual effects.
- **📱 Fully Mobile-Optimized:** Designed from the ground up for single-hand touch ergonomics, low RAM footprint, and battery efficiency.
- **🔮 Upcoming in v1.2.0:** Spotify account authorization, one-click playlist migration, and offline local audio scanning.

---

## 📥 Installation

1. Head over to the [Releases](../../releases) tab.
2. Download the latest `MusicNest-v1.1.0.apk`.
3. Open the APK on your Android device and confirm installation (allow installation from unknown sources if prompted).

---

## 🛠️ Tech Stack & Architecture

- **Platform:** Android
- **UI Framework:** Jetpack Compose / Modern Declarative UI
- **Language:** Kotlin
- **Architecture:** Clean Architecture + MVVM
- **Audio Engine:** Media3 / ExoPlayer
- **Network & Parsing:** Retrofit, OkHttp, KotlinX Serialization
