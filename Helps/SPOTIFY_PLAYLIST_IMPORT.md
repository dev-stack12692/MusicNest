# How to Import Spotify Playlists into MusicNest

Welcome to **MusicNest**! This guide walks you through importing any public Spotify playlist directly into your MusicNest library in just a few simple steps.

---

## Key Highlights

- **Universal Import:** You can import any playlist—whether it is your own curated mix, an algorithmic mix, or a public playlist created by another user or artist.
- **Full Library Sync:** All tracks, artist credits, and playlist sequencing are preserved and mapped into MusicNest.
- **Fast & Automated:** The import process begins immediately once the URL is verified.

---

## Step-by-Step Guide

### Step 1: Copy Your Spotify Playlist URL
1. Open the **Spotify** app (Desktop, Mobile, or Web Player).
2. Navigate to the playlist you wish to import.
3. Select the **three dots menu (`...`)** located beneath the playlist title or next to the play button.
4. Go to **Share** > **Copy link to playlist**.

---

### Step 2: Open MusicNest and Access the Library
1. Launch the **MusicNest** application or open your web dashboard.
2. In the main navigation bar, select the **Library** tab.
3. Locate the **Spotlight Scribe** option on your library dashboard.

---

### Step 3: Launch the Import Tool
1. Click the **Spotlight Scribe** button.
2. A dedicated playlist import modal / pop-up window will appear on your screen.

---

### Step 4: Paste and Import
1. Click inside the input field within the modal.
2. **Paste** the copied Spotify playlist URL (`Ctrl + V` on Windows/Linux or `Cmd + V` on macOS).
3. Click the **Import Playlist** button.

---

## What Happens Next?

- **Real-Time Processing:** MusicNest instantly parses the playlist URL and begins matching all track metadata.
- **Instant Availability:** Your newly imported playlist will appear directly under your **Library > Playlists** section once processing finishes.
- **Background Sync:** For larger playlists (100+ tracks), syncing may run seamlessly in the background while you continue listening to other music.

---

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

#### Do I need to own the Spotify playlist to import it?
> **No.** You can import any public Spotify playlist created by friends, creators, or Spotify editorial teams.

#### What if a song is missing or unavailable?
> MusicNest cross-references global track identifiers. In the rare event an exact master recording cannot be resolved, MusicNest will automatically suggest or link the closest high-quality alternate version.

#### Can I edit the playlist after importing?
> **Yes.** Once imported, the playlist becomes fully customizable within your MusicNest library. You can add tracks, rearrange order, or edit cover art and descriptions.
