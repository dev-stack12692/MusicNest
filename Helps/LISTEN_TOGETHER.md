# How the Listen Together Feature Works in MusicNest (v1.2.5)

The **Listen Together** feature in **MusicNest-v1.2.5** allows you to host a real-time synchronized music session so you and your friends can listen to the same tracks simultaneously.

---

> ### ⚠️ Important Network Notice
> **The Listen Together feature currently operates exclusively over LAN (Local Area Network).**  
> To connect, the host and all participants **must be connected to the exact same Wi-Fi network or mobile hotspot**. Support for **WAN (Wide Area Network)** to allow remote listening across different internet connections is currently under active development.

---

## Accessing Listen Together

You can access the Listen Together setup through two convenient paths:

1. **Quick Access (Home Screen):** Click the **two-person icon** located in the top-right corner of the Home screen, right next to your profile/account icon.
2. **Settings Menu:** Navigate to **Settings** and select the **Listen Together** option.

Clicking either option opens the **Listen Together** control modal.

---

## How to Host a Session

If you want to host the room and broadcast your playback:

1. Open the **Listen Together** modal.
2. Select **Host a Room**.
3. MusicNest will generate and display your unique **Room IP Address** (e.g., `192.168.x.x`).
4. Share this IP address with friends connected to your local network/hotspot.
5. Start playing any track—all joined friends will stay synchronized with your playback stream in real time.

---

## How to Join a Friend's Session

If your friend has already started a room:

1. Connect your device to the **same Wi-Fi network or mobile hotspot** as the host.
2. Open the **Listen Together** modal from the home screen or settings.
3. Select **Join a Room**.
4. *(Optional)* Change or set your **Display Name** in the designated field so the host and other listeners can identify you.
5. Enter the host’s provided **Room IP Address** into the required field.
6. Click **Join** to connect and begin synchronized listening immediately.

---

## Troubleshooting & Connectivity Checklist

- **Same Network Verification:** Ensure both devices are connected to the exact same router band (e.g., both on 2.4 GHz/5 GHz) or hotspot network.
- **Firewall Permissions:** Ensure MusicNest is allowed through the host's operating system firewall to accept local incoming connections.
- **IP Address Check:** Double-check that the IP address entered matches the numbers and dots shown on the host screen.
